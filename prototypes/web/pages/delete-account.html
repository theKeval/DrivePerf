Delete your DrivePerf account.

DrivePerf, made by [developer legal name], deletes your account and everything in cloud backup on request, whether or not you still have the app.

Request deletion

Enter the email on your DrivePerf account. We'll send a link to confirm it's you, so nobody else can delete your account.

Signed in with Apple and hid your email? Use the relay address ending in privaterelay.appleid.com.

If you have an active Pro subscription, cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account too. Deleting your account doesn't cancel it.

Check your email.

If there's a DrivePerf account for , we've sent a link to confirm the deletion. It expires in 24 hours.

Nothing arrived after a few minutes? Check spam, or try another address. You can also delete your account in the app.

Deletion confirmed.

We're deleting your account and cloud data now. It's usually done within 7 days and always within 30. We'll email you when it's complete.

What is deleted

  • Your accountSign-in, email, name and account ID.
  • Everything in cloud backupTrips with their routes, sensor data, scores, routes, cars and settings.
  • Ride-along pass recordsPasses you sent or redeemed.
  • Subscription link and diagnosticsYour customer record at RevenueCat, and crash data tied to your installation where the provider allows.

What is kept, and why

  • Purchase records at Apple or GoogleThey keep their own records of what you bought. We can't delete them; contact them directly.
  • A record of your requestThe date and outcome of the deletion, without your trip data, for [12 months], so we can show we honoured it.
  • Anonymous totalsCounts such as "drives decoded this week" that can't identify you.
