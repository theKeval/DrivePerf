Fact sheet

Product
DrivePerf: Drive Tracker, for iPhone and Android
In one line
Smooth is a skill. DrivePerf records your drives automatically and turns each one into a short Story, a replay with evidence and a Smoothness score against your own baseline.
For
People who love driving and do it every day, aged 18 and over
Launch
Early 2027 · Canada and the United States · English
Price
Free, with Pro at US$6.99 a month or US$39.99 a year (CA$8.99 / CA$49.99). Every new user gets a 14-day Pro Preview, no card
Privacy
Local-first, no account needed, no ad or tracking SDKs. Never sold, never shared with insurers
What it doesn't do
No speed contests, top-speed or 0–100 timing, leaderboards, streaks or insurance features. Nothing to look at while driving
Based in
Oakville, Ontario, Canada
