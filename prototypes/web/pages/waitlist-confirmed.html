While you wait

Smooth is a skill.

DrivePerf compares every drive with your own usual, so the score only goes one way if you do. A few things that help from day one:

  • Ease off earlierLong, gentle stops score better than late, firm ones.
  • Roll on, don't punch itSmooth starts read beautifully in the friction circle.
  • Mount your phoneA mounted phone gives the cleanest data. In a bag, we can't score fairly.
