Smoothness

Scored against you. Not against anyone else.

Smoothness measures how gently you brake, accelerate and turn, compared with your own usual on the same route. Your first three drives set the baseline. After that, every number comes with a reason.

    A full, gentle stop scores as well as not stopping, so rolling stops are never rewarded. There are no leaderboards and no streaks.

    All questions and support

    Will it drain my battery?

    We estimate about 2–4% an hour of driving with the screen off. Between drives, DrivePerf waits for the phone to tell it you're moving instead of keeping GPS on. We'll publish measured numbers from the beta.

    Is this an insurance app?

    No, and it never will be. Your data is never sold and never shared with insurers. DrivePerf is for people who enjoy driving and want to get smoother.

    Do I need an account?

    No. Everything in Free and the Pro Preview works without one. You only sign in if you want Pro cloud backup or a second device.

    What happens when the Pro Preview ends?

    You choose. Continue with Free and keep every trip summary forever, or keep Pro. Replay and Analysis older than 30 days are locked on Free, not lost.
