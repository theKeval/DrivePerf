DrivePerf prototypes

One screen engine, two platforms. Every screen is written once and rendered as iOS 26 Liquid Glass or Android 16 Material 3 Expressive from the shared fixtures. Open a platform, press c to toggle the catalog, and deep-link any screen and state.
