| 2026-11-07 | **Analytics and crash reporting (Gate 4, Keval's choice):** keep **Firebase Analytics and Remote Config**, and use **Sentry (EU data region)** instead of Crashlytics for crash and error reports. Reasons: Firebase Analytics is free at any scale, the industry default and already in the app; Sentry's official Kotlin Multiplatform SDK reports crashes from the shared Kotlin code on iOS, and it is independent of Firebase, so the VPS move does not affect it. Only one crash reporter is linked (two compete for the same crash hooks). Running PostHog next to Firebase Analytics was considered and rejected: double consent and store declarations, and two different numbers for the same metric. Sentry: `sendDefaultPii` off, no user set, IP storage off, allow-listed tags, initialised only after consent; free Developer plan at launch (5,000 errors/month, 30-day retention), Team plan (~US$26–29/month) when needed. Xcode Organizer and Android vitals are the consent-free cross-check. One opt-in in every country; no launch in China mainland or Russia; South Korea held for counsel. ADR-007 accepted; PRD F-ANA-03 and F-PRV-06, the data model, the privacy page and the design specs updated. | Keval, DRIVE-46 |
| 2026-11-09 | **Pipeline and scoring decisions (D1–D8 in `docs/architecture/pipeline-and-scoring.md`), all accepted as recommended.** (D1) Smoothness is an absolute 0–100 number, always shown as "±n vs your usual"; it is not normalised per driver. (D2) R1 stays at **25 Hz**, so ADR-004's R2 cost is unchanged; A19 confirms on real drives; resolves OQ-A11-04. (D3) Limited trips are scored and shown ("based on x of y min") but kept out of baselines and route records. (D4) "Was it needed?" applies to all three event types; a run with more than 2 "needed" marks cannot set a route record, though its trip score still updates; resolves OQ-22. (D6) The estimated start segment is never scored in v1. (D7) Suspected bus, train or bike trips nobody has answered are scored and shown, but held out of baselines and records until the user answers "No". (D8) No barometer for elevation in v1; revisit after A19. | Keval, DRIVE-56 |
