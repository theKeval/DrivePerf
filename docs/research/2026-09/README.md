# DrivePerf — research pack

**Revised 17 September 2026 · Android first · driveperf.com**

Start with the [updated main report](01-android-market-research.md). It is the single reading path for the current direction: **detailed analytics for driving enthusiasts, and efficiency/coaching for drivers seeking improvement**. Insurance apps are supporting measurement benchmarks only.

The report now includes DrivingTracker, Open Road, Zenroad, AutoBeacon, additional independent Android apps, specialist enthusiast tools and Cosworth PDR as a future comparison. The detailed evidence remains optional:

Public listings and reviews are desk-research evidence. No app was installed or accuracy-tested in this phase; sparse review coverage and naming ambiguities are recorded explicitly. Samples overlap where noted and must not be added together as unique users or reviews without deduplication.
